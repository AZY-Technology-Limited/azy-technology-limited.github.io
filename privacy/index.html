1. About this policy

This policy explains how AZY TECHNOLOGY LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with our company website at azy.hk.

It applies to this website and related enquiries. Our mobile applications and other products will have separate privacy notices where appropriate.

2. Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address and message. We use it to respond to your enquiry and manage related correspondence.

Providing this information is voluntary, but we may be unable to respond without sufficient contact details. This website does not provide account registration, online payments or contact forms.

3. Technical information and cookies

Our hosting provider may process routine server logs, including IP addresses, browser information, requested pages and access times, to deliver the website, maintain security and investigate technical issues.

The website itself does not set cookies, use browser storage, or include advertising trackers, analytics tools or third-party embedded content.

4. Use and disclosure

We use information for correspondence, website operation and security, and to meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use enquiry information for unsolicited marketing.

Hosting, email and other service providers may process information as needed to support these purposes. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including applicable legal requirements.

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your requests and questions

You may contact contact@azy.hk to request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you, or to raise a privacy question. You may also request deletion, subject to applicable law and any information we need to retain. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website or our practices. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
